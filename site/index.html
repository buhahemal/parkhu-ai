Gate funnel

What this means

Shows: how many symbols survive each hard filter (trend, RS, delivery, earnings window, etc.) and the keep-% vs the prior step.

Decide: if the funnel collapses early (e.g. trend/RS), the market breadth is weak — prefer fewer new ideas or stay flat. If a late gate drops most names (earnings / event risk), the process is working; don’t loosen gates to force trades.

Tip: “Tightest gates” chips highlight the biggest bottlenecks today.

Regime

What this means

Shows: Nifty/BankNifty direction, India VIX, FII vs DII flows, global cues (Asia/Europe/US), crude and USDINR.

Decide:

  • Bearish + high VIX / FII selling → smaller book risk, fewer fresh buys, respect stops.
  • Bullish + DII/FII supportive → OK to act on high-score ideas that cleared the funnel.
  • Mixed cues → wait for confirmation; don’t chase weak RS names.

Ideas

What this means

Shows: names that cleared all gates today — score, band (Buy/Watch), entry / stop / T1, R:R, hold-day estimate. No qty or capital here.

Decide: shortlist for Claude sizing. Prefer higher score + clean R:R ≥ 2 + sector not already crowded in the open ledger. Watch-band = monitor, don’t force entry.

Tip: Zero ideas is a valid outcome — do not invent trades outside the funnel.

Open ledger

What this means

Shows: live suggestions with entry vs last, P&L%, MFE (best excursion) and MAE (worst drawdown). Yellow “action” rows need attention first.

Decide:

  • Needs action (earnings, no data, stop threat) → reduce, exit, or stand aside before adding risk.
  • MAE deep / price near stop → plan invalidation; don’t average down blindly.
  • MFE strong but P&L faded → consider trailing / partial at T1 logic in Claude.

Sectors

What this means

Shows: how many idea + open names sit in each risk sector (counts only — not capital %).

Decide: avoid stacking more ideas into a sector that already dominates the open book. Concentration risk is a process call even before Claude sizes rupees.

Data quality

What this means

Shows: score coverage (% of KB weights that are live) and caveats (missing news, ownership, OHLC history, etc.).

Decide: if coverage is low or caveats are severe, treat scores as provisional — prefer stronger regimes and clearer technicals, and ask Claude to discount conviction.
